# Changelog

## Unreleased

- 0.7 (candidate): a licence id of a `license` has the shape of an SPDX idstring, its first character included (`curl`, `zlib-acknowledgement`, `bzip2-1.0.6`, `0BSD`): the rule « a capital first » is gone, for `read`, `LicenseExpression::parse` and the 0.7 schemas. `free`, `mit`, and `and`, `or` and `with` alone in lower case, are ids by their shape; the case of an id gets no warning and the text is given back as written. 0.4 to 0.6 and the table of 0.4 to 0.5 are unchanged: an id with a lowercase first stays refused there, and `NotConvertible` in a field the table judges. Nothing changes `PUBLISHED` (0.6).

- 0.7 (candidate): a `license` is an SPDX expression (ids, `WITH` an exception, `AND`, `OR`, parentheses), `public-domain`, `NOASSERTION` or `NONE`, in every field of a source, a sound and a firmware; `read` checks its shape, never the SPDX registry, and refuses another text as `BadValue` at the field (`AND`, `OR` and `WITH` alone are no longer ids). New export `LicenseExpression` (`parse`, `judge`), the one analysis of an expression. `convert_to` 0.7 moves a free `_sources` entry with such a licence to `sources`. 0.4 to 0.6 judge a `license` as before.

- 0.7 (candidate), **breaking** for a text of the earlier 0.7 candidate and for a struct literal of `Way`: a `sysex` way gives its numbers under their names, `Way` gaining `pr`, `p`, `c`, `r` and `id` (`Option<i64>`), and has no `ctrlIndex`, so `p` is never read as `pr`. `read` refuses a `sysex` way with none of the five (`BadValue` at the way), a `ctrlIndex` on a `sysex` way and any of the five on a way of a kind of MIDI (`UnknownField` at the field), a byte outside 0 to 255 or a number that is not an integer (`BadValue` at the field); a way with a kind its table does not take is refused for that first. A text of 0.4 to 0.6 reads as one `sysex` way with the numbers it gives, and `convert_to` 0.7 (and `resolve`) writes them so, a negative `id` alone included: it no longer writes the note `_flat`, `ways: []` or a `PartialControl` warning for it. A text of the earlier candidate is refused, and no converter rewrites it: convert it again from its source of 0.4 to 0.6. `ParamEntry` loses the hidden field `flat_id`. A parameter `id` that a published version admits, beyond 255 or negative, is no longer refused nor cut: an entry of `params` of 0.4 to 0.6 that gives it reads, writes back unchanged and converts to 0.7 as the `id` of its way, and one that gives `pr` alone is written back as `pr`, never as `id`. Nothing changes `PUBLISHED` (0.6).

- 0.7 (candidate): `resolve` and `resolve_assembly` take a `midi_of` file of 0.7 that names `role_vocabulary`, the state of its target (for a module, the MIDI accumulated) or any state when the target names none; the result holds that state and the roles of the file's own routes. Another state stays `BadValue` at `/role_vocabulary`, and a file before 0.7 stays refused.

- A control that one sound transfer places at places that differ (0.6 and 0.7) is no longer given by `sound_settings`, which took the first place: it is left out, so a comparison does not list it, and `warnings` reports `WarningKind::UnconfirmedPlace` at the `control` of each of these places (**breaking** for an exhaustive match). Identical places count as one; nothing is refused, and `convert_to` 0.7 carries every place.

- Holds state 2 of the vocabulary of roles beside state 1 (`VOCABULARY_STATES` is `1` and `2`, embedded through `osd/vocabulary-state-2.json`; not published): a descriptor of 0.6 or 0.7 with `role_vocabulary: "2"` reads. State 2 holds the settings `enabled` (on an oscillator, a track and the effects), `effect-type` (on the effects) and `modulation-depth` (on every effect, `effect`, `delay`, `reverb`, `chorus` and `equalizer`: how far the own movement of an effect goes), so `/Reverb/Enabled`, `/Effect/1/EffectType` and `/Reverb/ModulationDepth` read with it; `depth` keeps its meaning of state 1 and also applies to `delay` and `equalizer`, so the send holds for every effect too (`/Delay/Depth`, `/Equalizer/Depth`). A state holds a third type of term, `value` (**breaking** for an exhaustive match: `TermKind::Value`), a choice of a setting: `read_vocabulary` refuses a value without `applies_to` (`Missing`), with an empty one, one that names a term that is not a setting, and a value that writes `stands_under` or `at_top`; a role or a `paramPath` that writes a value as a part or a setting is `BadValue`. `Vocabulary::check_successor` now also refuses a next state in which a term loses an element of its `applies_to` or `stands_under`, or a part no longer opens a path. A `midi_of` file of 0.6 that names `role_vocabulary` is refused as before once its target is read: another state than the target's as `BadValue`, else as `UnknownField`; one of 0.7 is read (below).

## 0.6.0 - 2026-11-23

- A fingerprint of 0.6 names what it was taken on: exactly one of a `device` (which then carries its `firmware`) and the new `program` (`Program {name, version, kind}`, a firmware or an application as its card names it; `kind` is a firmware when absent). A fingerprint with neither is refused as `Missing` at `/device`, one with both as `BadValue` at `/program`, a device without `firmware` as `Missing` at `/device/firmware`; 0.4 and 0.5 refuse `program` as `UnknownField`. `convert_to` from 0.5 to 0.6 refuses a fingerprint that names no subject.

- The `kind` of a sound descriptor of 0.6 takes, outside its list, an extension `x-<registrant>-<term>`, kept as written (`SoundKind::Extension`); the reader treats it as a `sound` for its rules. A `kind` outside the list and outside the pattern stays refused (`BadValue`); 0.4 and 0.5 keep the closed list.

- `convert_to` from 0.5 to 0.6 tightens the three writings 0.6 tightens: a SysEx text in lower case is written in upper case (the same bytes); a control whose flat fields mix a CC, an NRPN and an RPN, and a sound content holding none of `sysex`, `midi`, `settings`, are refused as `NotConvertible`, at the control and at the content, because the converter chooses nothing and invents no content.

- In a block that a maker profile completes, every optional key accepts `null` (0.6), and the key of the profile does not pass into the resolved descriptor (`programs.dump: null` removes the profile's `dump`, `identity.ports: null` its `ports`); only a direct key of `identity` or `programs` does, never `profile`, and a `null` deeper stays refused. A `null` on a key the profile does not give is accepted and absent from the resolved descriptor. The cases of 0.4 and 0.5 are unchanged (see the entry above on their refusal of `null`). A conformance case holds `programs.dump: null`.

- The text of 0.6 lives at one address, `spec/0.6.md`, and the comments of the reader cite it and the rules of resolution (R1 and following) by number, instead of the files it replaced (`docs/INTERFACE.md`, `docs/controls.md`, `docs/vocabulary.md`); the text gives each requirement its normative word. The behaviour of the reader does not change.

- The complete descriptor of a device, without links: the new function `osd::complete_descriptor(&Resolved) -> Descriptor` derives from the result of `resolve` the device a client reads alone (no `midi_of`, no profile name in `identity` or `programs`, the capabilities of the merge); the word `osd resolve` of `osd-cli` writes it (see `osd-cli/CHANGELOG.md`). `resolve` and its conformance cases are unchanged. No schema and no case file changes.

- Another reader runs the conformance suite, which has its own version. `conformance/RUNNING.md` is the contract: a reader is an executable that takes one case folder and writes one JSON object (`result` or `fault`, and the other keys the case expects) on its standard output, and a runner compares it to the case and reports the version of the format and of the suite with the counts of passed, failed and skipped. The suite's version is `conformance/VERSION` (an addition is a minor number; a correction, a change or a removal a patch number), `conformance/SHA256SUMS` records each case file, and `conformance/optional.json` lists the cases with sound identities or Lua, which a runner skips and a claim of conformance names as « without the optional cases ». `osd` passes the suite through its adapter, the new word `osd conformance <case folder>` of `osd-cli`, which answers every kind of case (exit 2 for a folder that is not a case or a case of a kind it does not know). No schema and no case file changes.

- A published version is corrected by a patch version x.y.z, never in place: its own text `spec/<x.y.z>.md` and its own folder `schemas/<x.y.z>/`, recorded in `schemas/SHA256SUMS`, the folder of x.y unchanged; the same for a state of the vocabulary of roles, corrected by a new state. The rule is written once in `spec/ERRATA.md`, with its departure from OpenAPI and the owner's one departure, the correction in place of vocabulary state 1 (2026-11-21, entry below). `docs/CADRE.md` R6 cites it. No patch version is published, no schema and no conformance case changes.

- The repository says one truth about its versions: 0.6 is the published and frozen version and 0.5 is no longer the default, in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (§ 2 point 6), `osd/docs/INTERFACE.md` and the README, which gives one table of versions (date of the first commit of `schemas/<version>/`, status). The 0.6-draft entries below say "0.6, not frozen" and "`convert` keeps writing 0.5" as they were written; they are now superseded by this one.

- A program that runs alike on several devices (0.6, not frozen), on the owner's decision of 2026-11-22: the firmware kind `application` (a program installed on a host system of a device) joins the others; 0.4 and 0.5 refuse it (`BadValue` at `/kind`), as `module`. A program tied to the devices it runs on is a firmware descriptor with `targets`; `device.kind: software` stays for a program whose host machine is indifferent. `resolve_assembly` admits an `application` as the stock of an assembly and takes the MIDI of a stock target written `midi.same_as` as well as `midi.descriptor`, so each device the program runs on resolves to the program's MIDI; a module of kind `application` is refused. A device descriptor with a USB identity and no MIDI of its own (no `params`, `controls`, `commands`) is accepted, as a host for programs (existing behaviour, now written). Nothing published changes. Conformance cases: `firmware-application-on-two-devices`, `device-identity-without-midi-first`, `device-identity-without-midi-second`, `device-kind-software-host-indifferent`, `assembly-program-on-first-device`, `assembly-program-on-second-device`.

- What 0.6 freezes is exact and proven (0.6, not frozen): a hex text of a sound is upper-case hexadecimal and the content of a sound holds at least one of `sysex`, `midi`, `settings` (refused at the field, `BadValue`); the flat fields of a control and a control of a fingerprint never mix a CC, an NRPN and an RPN (`BadValue` at the control), and a fingerprint control knows `cc_lsb`, `rpn_msb`, `rpn_lsb`; the name of a resource is any non-empty name and `export` any non-empty name of a format (`RESOURCE_EXPORTS` is gone); the place of each control in a sound is `sound_transfers[].places` (not `settings`) and the coding of its data is `elements.data.coding` (not `packing`), the word of the `coding` of a file write step; `encode_chunk` codes bytes in `mcoded7` (M2-103-UM v1.2, 6.1.7) or `msb_first_7in8`. Cases in `conformance/0.6/read/` and `conformance/0.6/coding/`.

- The width of an NRPN or RPN value is a required field `value_bits` (0.6, not frozen), in the words of the norm: `7` (the data MSB alone) or `14` (the data MSB and LSB). It is on the flat fields of an entry and on each way of `also` (`CcEntry.value_bits`, `Way.value_bits`); any other width is refused at the field (`BadValue`), a missing one as `Missing`, and one on a way that gives neither an NRPN nor an RPN as `BadValue`; 0.4 and 0.5 refuse it as `UnknownField`. `convert_to` from 0.5 writes `14`. `bits` and `bits_per_byte` of a sound setting leave 0.6 (no descriptor held them and no case gave them a value). `docs/controls.md` states `relative` as the word of the norm and the channel of a control that neither the entry nor the table gives. Conformance cases: `nrpn-value-7-bits`, `nrpn-value-14-bits`, `rpn-value-7-bits`, `rpn-value-14-bits`, `nrpn-value-bits-missing-refused`, `nrpn-value-bits-word-refused`, `value-bits-on-a-cc-refused`, `transmit-recognize-relative`, and `nrpn-rpn-value-gets-14-bits` for the conversion.

- Versions that are compared follow one grammar (0.6), so two readers give the same answer: components separated by one dot, each a number then optional letters (`1.40C`, `2.0`, `10`; `1..2`, `B`, `.1`, `1.` and `0.3-beta` are outside it), in the pattern of `$defs/version` and in the reader alike. A sound's `min_version` follows it and is refused outside it (`BadValue`); a firmware's `version` and that of `based_on` stay free text, only displayed. `compare_versions` now returns `Option<Ordering>` and `version_in_target` returns `Fit::Unknown` when an input is outside the grammar, instead of an invented order; `judge_needs` leaves such a need `Unknown`. The departures from Debian (letters without case, `1.40` = `1.40.0`) are written in `docs/INTERFACE.md`. Cases in `conformance/0.6/read/` show it.

- A firmware target is `{device, midi, based_on, runs_on}` (0.6): the `midi` of a 0.6 firmware is written in each target (a `midi` at the first level is refused, `UnknownField`), `based_on` is the lineage only (`{name, version, url}`, one version; a `versions` there is refused) and `runs_on` (`RunsOn {name, versions}`, no `url`) says what the target runs on. `version_in_target` answers a `Fit`, `Yes`, `No` or `Unknown`: a target without `runs_on` versions is `Unknown`, which the caller decides. `resolve_assembly(id, device, files)` takes the device descriptor id it resolves for and reads each firmware through its target for it (`BadValue` at `/targets` when a firmware has none, or two; `device` may be `None` when the stock has one target). `convert_to` from 0.5 keeps `based_on` as the lineage, moves `midi` into the target and writes no `runs_on`. Cases in `conformance/0.6/` show it.

- An assembly pins the version of each module (0.6, not frozen, rewritten in place without a form kept): `stock` is an object `{firmware, version}` and `modules` a list of `{module, version}`, both versions required (`Missing` at `/stock/version`, `/modules/0/version`; a `stock` still text is `BadValue` at `/stock`), by the reader and by the schema. A sound's requirement is `needs` (`[{module, min_version?}]`) and no longer `modules`, which a 0.6 sound refuses as unknown; `made_on` keeps its name, and `made_on.stock` is the stock firmware of the sound's `device`. The firmware kind `module` (a plug-in applied to a stock firmware) joins `mod` (a whole modified firmware); 0.4 and 0.5 refuse it (`BadValue` at `/kind`), and `resolve_assembly` refuses a module of another kind or a stock that is not of kind `firmware` (`BadValue` at `/kind`). New `judge_needs(&Sound, &Assembly)`, `NeedJudgement`, `Verdict` (`Satisfied`, `NotSatisfied`, `Unknown`): a need is judged on the version the assembly pins, never on a catalogue descriptor, a version `compare_versions` cannot read leaves it unknown; a departure from the mature references, its reason written in `docs/INTERFACE.md`. Five conformance cases in `conformance/0.6/judge_needs/` show it (the new `judge_needs` kind of case); the cases of assemblies, of `needs` and of `made_on` are rewritten.

- Corrects, on 2026-11-21, a citation of the standard in the published schema of the vocabulary (`schemas/vocabulary/1/vocabulary.schema.json`) and in the reader's texts: the MIDI-CI Property Exchange Controller Resources are M2-117-UM v1.0, tables 6 and 12, as the schema now says (it named a wrong version and a wrong table). An erratum, on the owner's decision: the schema is adopted by no one yet, so it is corrected in place, an assumed departure from "a published version never changes". The schema is under checksum, which records the corrected file; no other published file changes.

- The reader of 0.4 and 0.5 refuses `null` on every direct key of `identity` and `programs` other than `profile` and `identity.firmware`, as the schemas of those versions do (R23): `BadValue` at that key (`/programs/dump`), in a descriptor and in a profile, by the version of the file that carries the `null`. `identity.firmware: null` stays read; 0.6 keeps `null` on every optional key. Four conformance cases in `conformance/0.4/resolve/` and `conformance/0.5/resolve/` show it.

- `resolve` refuses a sound-transfer setting whose `control` names an entry absent from the merged tables, even when the merged document keeps `midi_of`: `BadValue` at the pointer of the `control`, in the file asked for, the rule naming the absent entry. The check no longer depends on `midi_of`: it takes a "merged document" flag that `resolve` sets and that reading a text alone sets to false, so the text of a `midi_of` descriptor read alone is still judged on the pattern only. A control naming an entry of the target, of a target linked in turn, or added by the file itself stays accepted. One conformance case in `conformance/0.6/resolve/` shows it.

- Reads the assembly (0.6): `Kind::Assembly`, `Descriptor::Assembly`, `Assembly {stock, modules}`, a stock firmware and the modules applied to it, written by the builder of the firmware. `resolve_assembly(id, files)` gives its MIDI: the stock MIDI, then each module's own routes in the listed order, by the rules of `midi_of`; a module `same_as` the stock adds nothing and two modules on one address are not refused. `declared_kind` and `convert_to` know the kind; `Descriptor::schema` is public.

- Reads the builder of a module (0.6): optional `builder` (`Builder`: `name` and `url`, both required), the tool (Elekloader, Modwerk) that makes an assembly from the stock OS and modules, for every kind of firmware; the person flashes the result, and the builder stays the reference for building, installing and updating (OSD never rehosts). `install.method` may be `builder`, which requires `builder` (`Missing` at `/builder`); `steps` and `revert` are read as written. A builder without `name` or `url` is `Missing` at its pointer, one that is not an object or holds a `url` that is not text is `BadValue`; 0.4 and 0.5 refuse `builder` as `UnknownField`; `convert_to` from 0.5 adds none. Two conformance cases in `conformance/0.6/read/` show it.

- Reads the devices and base versions a firmware is made for (0.6): `targets`, a non-empty list replacing `device` and `based_on` for every kind of firmware (a 0.6 firmware holding either is refused, `UnknownField`). Each `Target` has a `device` and an optional `based_on` (`TargetBase`: `name`, `url`, `versions` as `BaseVersions`, a list or a `VersionRange` with `min` and `max`). `compare_versions(a, b)` orders two base versions (a number then letters without case per `.` component, a missing component 0: 1.40 < 1.40B < 1.40C < 1.41, 1.40 = 1.40.0) and `version_in_target(version, &target)` says whether one fits a target. `convert_to` turns a 0.5 firmware (and a 0.4 one) into one target, `based_on.version` becoming a list of one version.

- Reads how a file is written on a device by MIDI (0.6): `resources[].via.file_write.steps`, three steps `open`, `write` (played once per chunk, with `chunk_bytes` and `coding`) and `close`, each with its `send` (frame elements plus `text`, `file` and `captured`), its `reply` (frame elements plus `capture_json`), `reply_timeout_ms` and `delay_ms`; typed as `Step`, `StepKind`, `Coding`, `FileItem`, `NumberAs`, `SendToken`, `ReplyToken`. An unknown step, a list without one `open`, `write` and `close` or with a second of one, a null `chunk_bytes`, a reply without `reply_timeout_ms`, a timeout shorter than `delay_ms` and an unknown `coding` are refused at their pointer; a file without `steps`, 0.4 and 0.5 read as before. Eight conformance cases in `conformance/0.6/read/` show it, the Deluge from the community firmware.

- `resources[].export` accepts `knobkraft` (0.6 draft): `RESOURCE_EXPORTS` and the 0.6 schema list the new format of osd-formats, the skeleton of a KnobKraft Orm adaptation.

- Reads what a device can receive (0.6): `resources[]`, in the words of the ResourceList of MIDI 2.0 Property Exchange (`resource` starting with `X-`, `canSet` `none`, `partial` or `full`, `mediaTypes`), each with an `export` (a name of `RESOURCE_EXPORTS`, the closed list of the names of the formats of osd-formats, which a guard of osd-formats compares to `Format::ALL`) and an optional `via.file_write` (`path`, a list of segments: a fixed text, or a `variable` `vendor` or `device` with an optional `suffix`; `carried_by` `sysex`). `resource_path` resolves the path from a second descriptor, the one to place: the Minilogue on the Deluge gives `MIDI_DEVICES/DEFINITION/Korg/minilogue.xml`; a value that is empty or holds `/` or `\` is refused at the segment, naming the field of the source. A name without `X-`, a word outside the lists, a missing `export`, an empty path, a variable other than `vendor` and `device`, an unknown key or a way other than `file_write` are refused at their pointer; 0.4 and 0.5 refuse the field as `UnknownField`; `convert_to` from 0.5 adds none. Nine conformance cases in `conformance/0.6/read/` show it, the Deluge from the community firmware 1.3.

- A 0.6 file with `midi_of` may add what its target does not give and declare its capabilities, the list following the version of the file's own text read before any conversion: `params` with `entries` only (the 0.6 schema and the reader ask the three shape keys `get_frame`, `set_frame` and `value_type` only of a file without `midi_of`; `Params` holds them as options), added after the target's, which gives the keys (one given in the file is refused, `UnknownField` at `/params/<key>`; a `params` the target lacks must hold the three, else `Missing` at `/params/<key>`); scripted `custom_commands`; and `capabilities`, judged on the file's own text (a file that adds a script declares `["lua"]`, one that adds none does not), the resolved `capabilities` holding those of both files and `inherited` what only the target brings. A 0.5 file with `midi_of` keeps its limit, even over a 0.6 target: `capabilities` refused at `/capabilities`, `get_frame` in `params` at `/params/get_frame`; a 0.6 file over a 0.4 or 0.5 target is judged by the 0.6 rules. Eleven conformance cases in `conformance/0.6/resolve/` and two in `conformance/0.6/read/` show it.

- Reads a sound setting by setting (0.6): `sound_transfers[].settings`, a list where each setting points to a control (`control`, the JSON pointer of a `cc_params` or `params` entry) and gives its place in the data of the sound (unpacked, the name left out): whole bytes `{from, length}` (the value is their unsigned integer, the first byte the highest), or a Lua `script` (a place by bits or by 7-bit words is not a field of 0.6); a control carries no place. `sound_settings` gives the settings of a sound (`Setting {control, param_path, value}`, in the order of the controls); a control with no role or place, read by a script, or whose place the data does not hold, is absent, never guessed; a sound of a bank has the settings of a single sound of the same data. `compare_settings` sets two sounds side by side by role (`Comparison::Equal`, `Differ`, `Absent`; absent is never equal). A place past the data, an empty length, a place and a script together, a script without the `lua` capability and the capability without a script are refused at their pointer; 0.4 and 0.5 refuse `settings` as `UnknownField`; `convert_to` from 0.5 adds none. Seven conformance cases in `conformance/0.6/read/` show it.

- `resolve` joins a `midi_of` file and its target of two versions in the more recent of the two: the older text is converted (`convert_to`, forward only) before they are joined, each link compared with its own target, so the resolved document states one version and is never a document without `$schema` that carries the other's fields. A conversion that fails refuses the resolution in the file it fails in (`NotConvertible`); two files of one version resolve as before, and a `$schema` of an unread version is still refused at `/$schema`. The credit (`_sources`, `_midi_of_sources`) of a converted target is kept. Five conformance cases in `conformance/0.5/resolve/` show it.

- Reads what a virtual instrument stands for and how true it is (0.6): optional `device.stands_for` (the catalogue address `maker/device`) and `device.fidelity` (`faithful`, `modelled` or `principle`, the `Fidelity` words), only on a software device; the owner's decision of 2026-11-17, stated in `docs/INTERFACE.md` with its departure from MAME's grades. `fidelity` without `stands_for`, either field on a hardware device or one that gives no `kind`, a `stands_for` not of the form `maker/device` and a word outside the three are refused (`BadValue`) at the field; `resolve` refuses a `stands_for` absent from the catalogue at `/device/stands_for`, and accepts a software target. 0.4 and 0.5 refuse both as `UnknownField`; a plugin or a DAW of its own gives neither and reads as before; `convert_to` from 0.5 adds none.

- Prepares the publication of the reader and the command: the three crates (`osd`, `osd-formats`, `osd-cli`) share one version and their dependencies on one another from the workspace manifest, each carries the fields, the licence files and the README crates.io requires, and `cargo publish --workspace --dry-run` passes. A release workflow (`.github/workflows/release.yml`) builds `osd` for Linux x86_64, macOS arm64 and x86_64 and Windows x86_64, checks the three ways to install, then publishes the crates, attaches the binaries with `SHA256SUMS` and publishes the npm package `@roomi-fields/osd` (a launcher that downloads the binary of the platform and checks its checksum), by trusted publishing; `docs/RELEASE.md` says the order and what a failure does. Nothing is published by this change. The role vocabulary state 1 is read through a link inside the crate (`osd/vocabulary-state-1.json`) so that the package holds it.

- Reads a control reached by several ways as one control (0.6): the flat fields of a CC, NRPN or RPN entry are its first way, as in 0.5, and an optional list `also` gives the ways after it, each a `Way` with one message kind (`cc`, `cc` with `cc_lsb`, an NRPN or an RPN) and its own `range`; `CcEntry::ways` gives them in the order written, the flat way first, and never reorders them (a sender picks the first it can send, a receiver recognises all). A way of `also` that mixes two kinds or names none is refused at the way; a message (kind, channel, numbers; a 14-bit CC claims its CC number and its LSB number) that two ways or two controls claim is refused at the later place, the fault naming the earlier. 0.4 and 0.5 refuse `also` as `UnknownField` and still read two controls on one message; `convert_to` from 0.5 keeps each control and never joins two.

- Reads what in a message is the sound (0.6): `sound_transfers[]`, per form of transfer (`id`, `carries` `sound` or `bank`, `sound_id`, `match_frame`, `elements`, and `count` and `stride` for a bank), each element (`data`, `name`, `channel`, `device_number`, `header`, `checksum`, `bank_number`, `program_number`) found by positions `{from, length}` or by a Lua script, the data with its `packing` (`none`, `msb_first_7in8`, KORG minilogue xd, note 1). `sound_identity` computes the identity of a sound, the SHA-256 of its data element alone (unpacked; a name that lies inside the data, given by the new `name: {in_data: {from, length}}`, its place in the unpacked data counted from 0, is blanked first: its bytes set to zero, the length kept, so the same sound renamed has one identity even when the name straddles two packed groups; channel, device number, header, checksum, bank and program stay out), in 64 lowercase hexadecimal digits; `sound_identity_support` says at load, per transfer, whether it is computed without Lua (not when `data` is a script; a name given by a script is taken as outside the data, an `in_data` name on a data given by a script is refused). An element past the message, an empty data, a data over the header, the checksum or a transport part, two transport parts that overlap, positions and script together, a `packing` word outside the list, a name given by positions of the message that overlaps the data (only `in_data` places a name in the data), an `in_data` name past the unpacked data and an `in_data` mixed with `from`, `length` or `script` are refused at their pointer (the name's for the last four); the positions of the `settings` of a sound count in the unpacked data with the name in place, from 0; a script needs the `lua` capability. 0.4 and 0.5 refuse the field as `UnknownField`; `convert_to` from 0.5 adds none. `sha2` is now a dependency of the reader.

- Reads a control at the precision of MIDI 2.0 (0.6, MIDI-CI Property Exchange M2-117-UM v1.0, tables 6 and 12): optional `priority` (integer 1 to 5), `step_count` (integer, 2 or more), `transmit` and `recognize` (each `absolute`, `relative`, `both` or `none`) on a CC, NRPN or RPN entry and on a param entry, and `values` (named values) on a CC entry as on a param entry; `docs/controls.md` gives the table, property by property. A priority outside 1 to 5, a step count below 2 or not an integer, a word outside the four and a named value outside `range` (bounds included; compared to nothing without `range`) are refused at the pointer of the field (of the label for a value); 0.4 and 0.5 refuse the added fields as `UnknownField` and read the `values` of a param entry as before. `expects` keeps its six words. `convert_to` from 0.4 or 0.5 invents none.

- Reads the default value of a control (0.6): an optional integer `default` on a CC, NRPN or RPN entry and on a param entry, the raw value on an initialised sound, in the space of `range` (14 bits for a `cc` + `cc_lsb` pair, `range` as it is for `orientation: centered`). A default outside `range`, bounds included, or that is not an integer is refused at its pointer; without `range` it is compared to nothing; 0.4 and 0.5 refuse it as `UnknownField`. `convert_to` from 0.5 invents none. `osd-formats` carries the pencilresearch/midi CSV's `cc_default_value` and `nrpn_default_value` through it, both ways.

- Reads the role of a control (0.6) as a path: an optional object `role {parts: [{part, index?}…], setting}` on a CC, NRPN or RPN entry and on a param entry, one part or more and one setting, each a term of the vocabulary state the descriptor names in the new top-level `role_vocabulary`, or an extension `x-<registrant>-<term>`. `roles` gives each role as a `RoleEntry` (pointer, `RoleMeaning::Known` or `RoleMeaning::NoKnownRole`, its `paramPath` of MIDI-CI Property Exchange M2-117-UM, the role) and `role_from_param_path` reads a `paramPath` back to the same role. A role without `role_vocabulary`, a state the reader does not hold, an empty list of parts, the flat form `{part, index, setting}`, a term of the wrong type, a first part that does not open a path, a part that does not stand under the one before it, a setting that does not apply to the last part, a term neither known nor an extension and a role anywhere but on a control are refused at their pointer. `convert_to` from 0.5 adds none.

- Holds the states of the vocabulary of roles (`VOCABULARY_STATES`, `vocabulary_state`, `schemas/vocabulary/<state>/`; state 1 covers the defined controllers of MIDI 1.0 and the names of M2-104-UM table 22 that set a sound, and the parts and settings the standard leaves to each maker, each with the two devices of two makers that share it) and reads one with `read_vocabulary`: a name or a maker's word present twice, two terms of one `paramPath` segment, a term without a one-sentence definition, without a `source` or a `shared_by`, or a word without its origin, a `shared_by` of fewer than two makers, a `setting` without `applies_to`, a `stands_under` naming no part, and a term that composes a path of parts and a setting the state already holds are refused at the term. `Vocabulary::check_successor` checks that a state only adds. `resolve` refuses a `midi_of` file that names another state than its target's.

- Reads the 0.6 draft of the format and converts to it: `convert_to(text, kind, Version::V0_6)` carries a 0.4 or 0.5 descriptor to it, forward only. `convert` keeps writing the latest published version, 0.5, named by the new `PUBLISHED`; `osd convert --to 0.6` asks for the draft. A 0.6 device refuses a repeated `capabilities` entry, which 0.5 still reads once.

- `declared_kind` gives the kind that the `$schema` of a text declares (`None` when the text is not JSON, has no `$schema`, or one that is not an accepted address of a version read), so a command or a tool no longer keeps its own table of the addresses.

- `MidiType::template` gives the OSC address and arguments the specification fixes for a `midi_in` type (prefix not included), so an engine no longer keeps its own copy of the table; the 0.4 → 0.5 conversion reads it.

- `resolve` accepts `$schema` in a `midi_of` file and keeps the target's in the result: `convert` writes it in every 0.5 file, so a converted `midi_of` driver was refused. A key that belongs to the target is still refused; a `$schema` that is not a 0.5 schema URL is refused at `/$schema`.

- Reads the closed vocabularies of 0.5 (`decode`, `expects`, `selects`, `_sources[].type`, `_sources[].license`) and refuses a word outside a list unless it is a declared extension `x-<registrant>-<term>`; `convert` maps the makers' words of 0.4 by the tables of `README.md`.

- Reads OSD 0.4 strictly: device, maker profile, sound, fingerprint and firmware descriptors into their types, documentary fields included, and writes them back unchanged.
- Reads an integer by its value, as JSON Schema does: `1.0` and `1e2` are the integers 1 and 100, written back as `1` and `100`; a number with a fraction where an integer is expected stays refused.
- Resolves a driver's shared MIDI (`midi_of`) and maker profiles through a `Files` source, as osc-bridge 0.16 does; refusals are typed faults with their file and JSON pointer.
