# Changelog of the format

One entry per version of the format, the newest first: what the version changed, and what a descriptor of the version before becomes. The text of a version is in `spec/`; a published version never changes.

Each change names the cases of `conformance/<version>/convert` that prove it, or says "no conversion case" where it has none.

**What the reader reads.** The reader reads 0.4, 0.5, 0.6 and 0.7 (candidate), and converts from 0.4 to 0.5, then to 0.6, then to 0.7. 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 are not read.

## State 2 of the vocabulary of roles (not published)

A state of the vocabulary is not a version of the format: it changes no schema of a version, and no descriptor needs converting. Its text is `spec/vocabulary/2.md`; `schemas/SHA256SUMS` records none of its files until the owner publishes it.

- **New type of term**: `value`, a choice of a selector, whose `applies_to` names settings. Cases: `conformance/0.6/vocabulary/`.
- **New terms**: the parts `track`, `sample`, `loop`, `slice`, `kick`, `snare`, `clap`, `hi-hat`, `tom` and `cymbal`; the settings `start`, `end`, `filter-type`, `enabled` (whether a part is on or off), `effect-type` (which effect a part runs) and `modulation-depth` (how far the own movement of an effect goes; `depth` keeps its meaning of state 1, the send to an effect, and `level` stays the loudness of a part; the send and that movement hold for every effect: `effect`, `delay`, `reverb`, `chorus` and `equalizer`); the waveforms `sine`, `triangle`, `sawtooth`, `square` and `noise-wave`; the filter types `low-pass`, `high-pass`, `band-pass` and `band-reject`.
- **Terms of state 1**: unchanged, but for the parts `filter`, `amplifier`, `envelope` and `lfo`, which also stand under the new parts the text lists, and the settings `level`, `pan` and `pitch`, which also apply to them, and `depth`, which also applies to `delay` and `equalizer`. Every role of state 1 reads in state 2.
- **Rule of entry**: a function no existing term or composition names, with no threshold of makers.
- **Draft for 0.8**: [proposal 0020](proposals/0020-a-named-value-carries-a-term-of-the-vocabulary.md), a named value carries a value term. No conversion case.

## 0.7 (candidate, not published)

In the public repository as a candidate while its proposals are candidates: it is not accepted and may change before its acceptance. Until the owner accepts them, its schema addresses and its book pages are not served. Source: `spec/0.7.md`, a copy of `spec/0.6.md`. 0.7 opens with its change of address alone.

- **Address**: the `$id` and `$schema` of each file name `0.7`; a 0.6 file takes the 0.7 `$schema` and nothing else changes; a 0.4 or 0.5 file reaches 0.7 through 0.6, but for a control that mixes kinds of message (below). Cases: `0-6-device-changes-only-its-schema`.
- **A licence is an SPDX expression** ([proposal 0024](proposals/0024-a-licence-is-an-spdx-expression.md)): every `license` (of a source, a sound, a firmware) admits `WITH` an exception, `AND`, `OR` and parentheses, beside one id, `public-domain`, `NOASSERTION` or `NONE`; a reader and the schemas check its shape, never the SPDX registry, and a reader alone counts the parentheses. 0.4 to 0.6 judge it as before; a text of 0.6 converts with its licence unchanged (`AND`, `OR` and `WITH` alone are no longer ids), and a free `_sources` entry whose licence is an expression goes to `sources`. No conversion case; read cases `license-*` under `conformance/0.7/read`.
- **A licence id may start in lowercase** (proposal 0024, amended): an id of a `license` has the shape of an SPDX idstring, its first character included (`curl`, `zlib-acknowledgement`, `bzip2-1.0.6`, `0BSD`); the rule « a capital first » is gone from the reader and the schemas, which still never read the SPDX registry, and a descriptor writes an id as the SPDX list spells it, which no reader checks. 0.5 and 0.6, and the table of 0.4 to 0.5, keep the former shape (`NotConvertible` in a field the table judges). Tests that refused `mit` or `free` in a 0.7 text now accept them: in `osd`, `license_expressions`, `credit_of_every_kind_0_7`, `profile_credit_0_7` and `sources_0_7`; in `osd-formats`, `source_refused`; in `osd-cli`, `import_source_refused`. No conformance case refused them. No conversion case; read case `license-lowercase-id-accepted` under `conformance/0.7/read`.
- **A control with no message** (proposal 0009): `ways` of an entry of `cc_params` or `params` is a list of zero or more ways. `ways: []` says the message is not known yet: such an entry claims no message and alone carries `range`, `transmit`, `recognize` and, in `cc_params`, `nrpn_lsb_range` (`UnknownField` beside a way); its reference scale is its `scale`, else its `range`. A reader warns of it, never refuses it. An entry of 0.4, 0.5 or 0.6 with no message (a `params` entry with a `range` and no number too) is read the same way, and the converter writes it with `ways: []`, these fields unchanged. Cases: `control-without-a-way-warned` and `params-entry-without-a-way-warned` (read), `transmit-beside-a-way-refused` (read), `entries-without-a-message-keep-their-fields`.
- **The numbers of a `sysex` way** (proposal 0009): a `sysex` way gives its numbers under their names, the fields `pr`, `p`, `c`, `r` (0 to 255) and `id` (any integer, negative too), at least one, with no `ctrlIndex`. The converter writes each number the 0.6 entry gives under its name, a negative `id` alone too, and no note `_flat`. **Breaking** for a text of the earlier 0.7 candidate, whose `ctrlIndex` on a `sysex` way is refused: it is converted again from its source of 0.4 to 0.6. Cases: `sysex-way-with-id-beyond-a-byte-accepted`, `sysex-way-with-negative-id-accepted`, `sysex-way-byte-above-255-refused`, `sysex-way-byte-below-0-refused`, `sysex-way-byte-not-an-integer-refused`, `sysex-way-id-not-an-integer-refused`, `sysex-way-without-a-number-refused`, `ctrlindex-on-a-sysex-way-refused`, `sysex-number-on-a-way-of-midi-refused` (read), `params-p-c-r-keep-their-names`, `params-pr-alone-keeps-its-name`, `params-id-beside-pr-keeps-both`, `params-id-beyond-a-byte-becomes-the-id-of-the-way`, `params-negative-id-alone-becomes-the-id-of-the-way`.
- **A control that mixes kinds of message** (proposal 0009): a control of `cc_params` of 0.4 or 0.5 whose flat fields mix a CC, an NRPN or an RPN, which 0.6 refuses, becomes one control with one way per message, in the order CC, NRPN, RPN, numbers kept, an NRPN or an RPN of 14 bits, `range` on every way and `nrpn_lsb_range` on the NRPN way; a message of the mix not fully known goes to the note `_incomplete` (below). The converter refuses it when it gives an `nrpn_lsb_range` without an NRPN and when one of its messages is claimed twice. A control of a fingerprint that mixes kinds stays refused, and so does the conversion to 0.6. Cases: `mixed-cc-and-nrpn-control-of-0-4-becomes-two-ways`, `mixed-cc-and-nrpn-control-of-0-5-becomes-two-ways`, `mixed-cc-and-rpn-control-becomes-two-ways`, `mixed-nrpn-and-rpn-control-becomes-two-ways`, `mixed-three-kinds-control-becomes-three-ways`, `mixed-control-with-nrpn-of-one-number-kept-as-note`, `mixed-control-with-cc-lsb-without-cc-kept-as-note`, `mixed-control-with-block-without-nrpn-refused`, `mixed-control-message-claimed-twice-refused`, `fingerprint-control-mixing-cc-and-nrpn-refused`, `fingerprint-name-control-mixing-cc-and-nrpn-refused`.
- **A message not fully known**: an NRPN or an RPN given by one number, and a `cc_lsb` without its `cc`, of 0.4, 0.5 or 0.6, have no way: the converter writes no number the text does not give and refuses nothing for them. It keeps them in the note `_incomplete` of the entry (`_incomplete_2`, `_incomplete_3`… when the key is taken); a control left with no way is `ways: []`, partial and warned. A reader gives them no way in a text of an older version either. Cases: `nrpn-of-one-number-kept-as-note`, `rpn-of-one-number-kept-as-note`, `cc-lsb-without-cc-kept-as-note`.
- **The block and the range of a control**: the `nrpn_lsb_range` of a control of `cc_params` that has a way goes on the `nrpn` way of the flat fields, else, when they give no NRPN number, on the single `nrpn` way of `also`; when no way can receive it, the converter keeps it on the entry as the note `_nrpn_lsb_range` (`_nrpn_lsb_range_2`, `_nrpn_lsb_range_3`… when the key is taken). The `range` of a control of 0.6 with no flat message and a non-empty `also`, of a control that has a way, which no way takes, is kept unchanged on the entry as the note `_range` (`_range_2`, `_range_3`… when the key is taken) and goes to no way; with a `default`, `values` or `step_count`, written in that `range`, the converter writes it as `scale` when the most precise way has another scale, and no `scale` otherwise; a control left with no way keeps its `range` on the entry. A control of 0.4 or 0.5 that mixes kinds of message follows the entry « A control that mixes kinds of message ». Cases: `nrpn-block-beside-a-cc-of-0-4-kept-as-note`, `nrpn-block-beside-a-cc-of-0-5-kept-as-note`, `nrpn-block-beside-a-cc-of-0-6-kept-as-note`, `nrpn-block-goes-to-the-only-nrpn-way-of-also`, `nrpn-block-beside-also-without-nrpn-kept-as-note`, `nrpn-block-beside-two-nrpn-ways-of-also-kept-as-note`, `nrpn-block-note-takes-the-next-free-key`, `range-beside-also-alone-stays-a-note`, `range-beside-also-alone-gives-the-scale-of-a-default`.
- **Title and description** (proposal 0007): a control, a `params` entry, a command and a trigger accept `title` (never empty) and `description`; a control accepts `value_description` and `notes`. The converter moves `_usage`, `_name`, `_description` and a control's `_notes` to them when the value is a string and the field is absent, and writes no `title` otherwise. Cases: `usage-becomes-value-description`, `usage-name-description-notes-become-fields`, `usage-without-values-still-becomes-value-description`, `usage-with-existing-value-description-keeps-both`, `name-with-existing-title-keeps-both`, `entry-without-name-gets-no-title`, `text-of-wrong-form-stays-under-its-underscore`, `trigger-description-becomes-description`.
- **Contexts** (proposal 0003): a device declares `contexts`; a way of `cc_params` carries `in` and `slice`; a message is claimed once by claims that can hold together, by the one rule of coordinates. The converter writes none (it invents no value). No conversion case; read cases under `conformance/0.7/read` (contexts, in, slice, midi-of-file-alone).
- **Who supplies a source** (proposal 0010): a source accepts `provider`, the identifier of an entry of the table of providers (`schemas/providers.json`, its own version, a date); a reader refuses a malformed one and warns of one its table does not hold. The four source types named after a product leave the list of `type`, which keeps its ten terms. The converter writes `contributed` for `electra-preset`, `pencilresearch`, `knobkraft` and `midnam`, with `provider` `electra-one`, `pencilresearch` and `knobkraft` (none for `midnam`) and `type in 0.6: <word>` as the first line of `notes`; a well-formed `provider` already there is kept, another value goes under `_provider`; the ten other terms are unchanged. Cases: `source-product-types-become-contributed`, `source-evidence-type-unchanged`, `source-provider-already-there`, `source-product-keys-become-general-fields`.
- **The credit of every kind**: a maker profile, a sound, a fingerprint, a firmware and an assembly (the `made_on` of a sound included) accept `sources`, the list of sources of a device, judged by the same rules, and a reader refuses their `_sources`. A source accepts `profile`, a string: the maker profile it comes from. Since their `_sources` was free before 0.7, the converter judges it entry by entry and refuses nothing for it: each entry that is a source of 0.7 once carried as a device's goes to `sources`, an object without `type` as `contributed` with `no type in 0.6` first in `notes`; every other entry, and a `_sources` that is no list, is kept under the note `_sources_2` (the next free number), which a reader warns of as not carried as credit. A `profile` that is not a string goes under `_profile`. From 0.4, a word of a profile that the tables of 0.4 to 0.5 do not map stays refused as `NotConvertible`; `_midi_of_sources` of a profile stays a note. Cases: `profile-sources-key-renamed`, `profile-0-4-source-words-become-terms`, `sound-sources-renamed`, `sound-made-on-sources-renamed`, `fingerprint-sources-renamed`, `firmware-sources-renamed`, `assembly-sources-renamed`, `source-without-type-becomes-contributed`, `sources-entries-kept-as-note`, `sources-not-a-list-kept-as-note`, `sources-note-takes-the-next-free-key`, `source-profile-not-a-string-kept-as-note`; read cases under `conformance/0.7/read` (`*-sources-read`, `*-underscore-sources-refused`, `sound-sources-not-carried-warned`, `source-profile-not-a-string-refused`).
- **The credit of the profiles of a resolution**: a resolution whose result is 0.7 appends the sources of every profile of each block's chain, each marked with `profile`: to `sources` for a block the file writes, to `midi_of_sources` for a block of a `midi_of` target (rules 23.42 and 23.43). A source already credited is not repeated, one whose `id` is taken is given without it, and a reading never names a source the resolution adds. No conversion case; cases under `conformance/0.7/resolve` (`profile-sources-*`, `profile-source-*`, `assembly-module-over-credited-midi-no-repeat`).
- **The number of a command** (proposal 0008): the converter writes `id` on a command whose `_electra.parameter` is of `type` `sysex` with an integer `number`, and removes that `_electra` only when it says nothing more; one that carries one more key, beside `parameter` or inside it, stays whole as a note under `_electra`. Any other shape stays a note, with no `id`. Case: `electra-number-becomes-id`.
- **The source of a reading** (proposals 0006 and 0008): a file without `midi_of` read without being resolved judges it against its `sources` and `midi_of_sources`, an entry with `profile` included; a `midi_of` file read alone is not refused for it; in a resolution, a file without `midi_of`, the end of a chain included, is first judged on its own sources, kept or discarded readings alike, and refused in that file; each reading of a `midi_of` file that the joined device keeps is judged against the sources written in the file asked for and its targets, without those added from profiles, and refused in the file asked for (5.3.4, 23.43). The reader already did so: no descriptor changes. No conversion case; cases `read/reading-source-of-midi-of-sources-accepted`, `read/midi-of-file-alone-reading-source-unknown-accepted`, `resolve/midi-of-chain-reading-names-a-source-of-the-file-asked-accepted`, `resolve/midi-of-chain-kept-reading-names-no-source-refused` and `resolve/midi-of-chain-end-reading-names-a-source-of-the-file-asked-refused` under `conformance/0.7`.
- **The date of a source** (proposal 0008): 0.7 has no field `imported_at` in a source, and a reader refuses it in `sources` and in `midi_of_sources`. The converter keeps its value as the note `_imported_at` of the same source, in `sources` and `midi_of_sources`; when that key is taken, under `_imported_at_2`, then the next number. Cases: `source-imported-at-becomes-a-note`, `midi-of-source-imported-at-becomes-a-note`, `removed-fields-dropped`.
- **Port names and the signs of MIDI 2.0** (proposal 0004): an entry of `identity.ports` is a rule (`operator`, `value`, optional `direction`, `platform`, `stack`); `identity.endpoint_name` is a rule; a fingerprint says its `platform`, `stack`, `endpoint_name` and `product_instance_id`; the matching of a rule against a fingerprint and the comparison of two product instance ids are defined (5.3.7). The converter writes each plain name as a `contains` rule, drops an empty one, and writes neither sign. A resolution with a 0.7 profile or device converts the older texts before merging them (23.22). Cases: `port-name-becomes-contains-rule`, `profile-port-names-become-rules`, `empty-port-name-is-removed`, `profile-empty-port-name-is-removed`, `only-empty-port-names-leave-an-empty-list`, `ports-null-stays-null`, `fingerprint-changes-only-its-schema`.
- **`midi_of`**: a 0.6 file and its 0.7 target, or the reverse, are joined in 0.7 (rule 23.22). In a chain, what each file may hold is judged on the text it writes, in its own version (rules 23.2 and 23.9): a 0.6 file between a 0.7 file and its target keeps its `_sources`; the credit it writes under `_midi_of_sources` follows its target's in `midi_of_sources` (rule 23.13). Cases: `midi-of-0-6-file-on-0-7-target`, `midi-of-0-7-file-on-0-6-target`, `midi-of-chain-of-0-6-files-under-a-0-7-file`.
- **Who a direction is said of**: `transmit` and `recognize` are read from the point of view of the device or the software described, as M2-117-UM says them (5.3.3): a tool that sends to the device takes the first way whose `recognize` is not `none`, a tool that listens to it recognises every way whose `transmit` is not `none`. The claims and the refusals do not change. No conversion case.
- **The transport of a software** (5.3.8): the text and the schema say what `kind`, `host`, `port`, `reply_port` and `passthrough_prefix` of `device.transport` are: without `transport` the software is reached by MIDI, `kind` decides whether a command goes by its `frame` or its `forward`, and 0.7 does not define what is sent of a command that writes only the form of the other transport. No reader changes what it refuses. No conversion case.
- **A control placed twice in one sound transfer** (12.1): places that name the same control and differ are both kept, the control is no setting of a sound of that transfer, and a reader warns of each (`UnconfirmedPlace`). Identical places count as one. The converter carries every place. Cases: `control-placed-twice-in-a-transfer-carried`, and `control-placed-twice-in-a-transfer-accepted` (read).
- **A setting neither sound has** (12.3): the example no longer says `absent` for a control with no place, which the comparison does not list. No conversion case.
- **The roles of a `midi_of` file** (23.9, 23.39): a `midi_of` file of 0.7 may name `role_vocabulary` when it is the state of its target, or any state when the target names none; for the MIDI file of a module the target is the MIDI accumulated. Another state stays refused (23.35), and a file of 0.6 stays refused. Cases (resolve): `midi-of-own-roles-under-the-target-state`, `midi-of-own-roles-over-a-target-without-state`, `midi-of-role-vocabulary-state-differs-refused`, `assembly-modules-name-one-role-state`, `assembly-modules-name-two-role-states-refused`.
- **What a module changes** (23.37, 23.39): the MIDI file of a module writes routes and the other keys a `midi_of` file may hold; its `identity` and `programs` blocks replace those accumulated, whole, and the `device` block stays the stock's. The text now says what the reader does. Case (resolve): `assembly-module-replaces-identity-and-programs`.
- **The element alone** (8.1): 0.7 does not define what `{"arg": name}` writes for a value below 0, as above 127. No conversion case.

## 0.6

Published 2026-11-17 as a draft, frozen with the checksums of its schemas in `edfdffe`. Source: `spec/0.6.md`, section 7 and Annex A. What a 0.5 file becomes is what the converter writes; a 0.5 file that uses nothing listed changes by its `$schema` alone. The cases are in `conformance/0.6/convert`.

- **A fingerprint names its subject** (`device` or `program`): a 0.5 fingerprint converts when its device holds its firmware, and is refused without a device or without the firmware. Cases: `fingerprint-named-device-converts`, `fingerprint-without-device-refused`, `fingerprint-device-without-firmware-refused`.
- **Firmware `targets`**: a 0.5 firmware becomes one target; its first-level `device`, `midi` and `based_on` are refused in 0.6. Cases: `firmware-device-becomes-target`, `firmware-0-4-device-becomes-target`, `firmware-midi-moves-into-the-target`.
- **An NRPN or RPN says its width** (`value_bits`): a 0.5 one becomes 14 bits. Case: `nrpn-rpn-value-gets-14-bits`.
- **One spelling**: a hex text is upper-case; a control never mixes a CC, an NRPN and an RPN; a sound holds at least one of `sysex`, `midi`, `settings`. Cases: `hex-lowercase-becomes-uppercase`, `control-mixing-cc-and-nrpn-refused`, `fingerprint-control-mixing-cc-and-nrpn-refused`, `fingerprint-name-control-mixing-cc-and-nrpn-refused`, `sound-without-content-refused`.
- **Address**: the `$id` and `$schema` of each file name `0.6`; a 0.5 file takes the 0.6 `$schema`. No conversion case.
- **`capabilities`** forbids a repeated entry; the converter writes it once. No conversion case.
- **Null keys** on `identity` and `programs` drop what the maker profile gives. No conversion case.
- **Roles**: a control may carry a `role`; a 0.5 file has none, none is added. No conversion case.
- **Controls**: `default`, `priority`, `step_count`, `transmit`, `recognize`, `values` of a CC, `also`; a 0.5 file gains none. No conversion case.
- **Banks and dumps**: `writable` on a bank, `reply_timeout_ms` on a dump. No conversion case.
- **Resources**: `resources` of a device (the Deluge file write). No conversion case.
- **Software devices**: `stands_for` and `fidelity`. No conversion case.
- **Sounds**: `external_ids`, `needs`, `made_on`, sound transfers (`sound_transfers`, `places`, `coding`); a 0.5 sound gains none. No conversion case.
- **Open lists**: any name of a resource, any non-empty `export`. No conversion case.
- **Sound `kind`**: an extension `x-<registrant>-<term>` beside the nine words. No conversion case.
- **Firmware kinds** `module` and `application`, and a `builder`. No conversion case.
- **Host device without MIDI**: a device with a USB identity and no MIDI of its own is valid. No conversion case.
- **Versions compared** follow one grammar. No conversion case.
- **Assembly**: a new document; a 0.5 file has none. No conversion case.
- **`midi_of`**: a file that follows a target adds only `params` entries. No conversion case.

Taken out of 0.6: nothing that 0.5 published.

## 0.5

Published 2026-10-30, with the checksums of its schemas in `814f9ac`. Source: `spec/0.6.md`, section 7 (and 8.3, 9.4). A converter converts every 0.4 descriptor to 0.5 as follows. The cases are in `conformance/0.5/convert`.

- **`$schema`**: the first key, an address under `https://w3id.org/osd/0.5/`, required. No conversion case.
- **Frame elements**: each `{placeholder}` string becomes a structure; `{checksum}` without an algorithm and `{function}` are refused. Cases: `frame-commands`, `frame-identity`, `frame-params`, `frame-profile`, `frame-programs-before`, `frame-programs-dump`, `frame-replies`, `fault-checksum-without-algorithm`, `fault-checksum-twos-without-7-bit-suffix`, `fault-function`.
- **`pre`**: an expression string becomes a tree of operations. Case: `pre-tree`.
- **Addresses**: a string with `{name}` becomes a list of segments; a malformed brace is refused. Cases: `address-segments`, `fault-brace-empty`, `fault-brace-unclosed`.
- **`forward.args` and `emit_osc`**: a `{name}` becomes an `arg` or `capture` object. Cases: `forward-args`, `emit-args`.
- **`midi_in`**: the object of templates becomes the list of its types; a non-canonical template is refused. Cases: `midi-in-list`, `midi-in-notes`, `midi-in-empty`, `fault-midi-template`.
- **Closed vocabularies**: `decode`, `expects`, `selects`, `_sources[].type` and `_sources[].license` take their terms from closed lists; a word outside the tables is refused. Cases: `vocabularies-merged`, `license-none`, `fault-selects-case`, `fault-encoding-lsb-msb`.
- **`capabilities`**: derived from the fields present, absent when empty. Case: `capabilities-lua`.
