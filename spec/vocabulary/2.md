# The vocabulary of roles, state 2

## 1 Scope

State 2 of the vocabulary of roles is written as data under `schemas/vocabulary/2/`: `terms.json` and its schema `vocabulary.schema.json`. It holds every term of [state 1](1.md) and adds what describes drum machines and samplers, the choices of the waveform and filter type selectors, whether a part is on or off, the type of an effect and how far its own movement goes, so that a waveform, a filter type, a drum voice and the loop of a sample say the same on every device. A descriptor of 0.6 or later names it with `role_vocabulary` (`"2"`).

> NOTE State 2 is not published: `schemas/SHA256SUMS` records none of its files ([ERRATA.md](../ERRATA.md), section 1.2). Publishing it is the owner's decision, after the proposal and the delay of [PROCESS.md](../PROCESS.md).

## 2 Conformance

The classes and the key words are those of [the text of state 1](1.md), section 2.

What that text says of a role, of its `paramPath`, of a term and of what a reader checks holds for state 2, with what this text adds. Two things of that text change here: a third type of term, `value` (section 3), and the rule of entry (section 8), which replaces its section 9 for the terms a publisher enters from this state on.

State 2 only adds to state 1: each term of state 1 keeps its type, its definition, its `source` and its `replaced_by`, and every element of its `words`, `shared_by`, `applies_to` and `stands_under`. A part that opens a path in state 1 still opens one. So a role of state 1 reads in state 2 with the same `paramPath`.

## 3 The type `value`

A term has one of three types:

| `type` | Is | `applies_to` names |
|---|---|---|
| `part` | a part of the instrument | nothing: the field is absent |
| `setting` | what is set on a part | parts of the state |
| `value` | a choice of a setting that selects | settings of the state |

A publisher MUST give a term of type `value` an `applies_to` that names the settings of the same state it is a choice of.

A reader MUST refuse a value without `applies_to`, as `Missing` at `applies_to`.

A reader MUST refuse a value whose `applies_to` is empty, as `BadValue` at `applies_to`.

A reader MUST refuse a value whose `applies_to` names a term that is not a setting of the state, as `BadValue` at that name.

A reader MUST refuse a value that writes `stands_under` or `at_top`, as `BadValue` at that field.

A reader MUST refuse a setting whose `applies_to` names a term that is not a part of the state, a value included.

A value is neither a part nor a setting of a role.

A reader MUST refuse a value written as a part or as the setting of a role, as `BadValue` at that field.

A reader MUST refuse the segment of a value in a `paramPath`, at its position.

> NOTE The schema of state 2 holds the shapes: the three types, `applies_to` for a setting and for a value, no `stands_under` and no `at_top` on either. What a name refers to is the reader's to judge. How a descriptor says which value a choice of a control is, no version of the format says yet.

## 4 The new terms

Each term is defined in `schemas/vocabulary/2/terms.json`, with its `source` or its `shared_by`.

### 4.1 Parts

| Term | Is | Source or devices that share it |
|---|---|---|
| `track` | a lane of the instrument with its own sound | Elektron Digitakt II, Novation Circuit Rhythm |
| `sample` | a recording the instrument plays back | SFZ, opcode `sample` |
| `loop` | the portion of a sample that playback repeats | SFZ, opcodes `loop_start`, `loop_end` |
| `slice` | one of the portions a sample is cut into | Elektron Digitakt II, Novation Circuit Rhythm |
| `kick` | the bass drum | General MIDI percussion key map, keys 35, 36 |
| `snare` | the snare drum | General MIDI percussion key map, keys 38, 40 |
| `clap` | a clap of hands | General MIDI percussion key map, key 39 |
| `hi-hat` | the closed, open and pedal cymbals | General MIDI percussion key map, keys 42, 44, 46 |
| `tom` | a tom drum, low to high | General MIDI percussion key map, keys 41, 43, 45, 47, 48, 50 |
| `cymbal` | a cymbal other than the hi-hat | General MIDI percussion key map, keys 49, 51, 52, 55, 57, 59 |

The last six are called the drum instruments below.

### 4.2 Settings

| Term | Is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `start` | where playback begins | SFZ, opcodes `offset`, `loop_start` |
| `end` | where playback stops | SFZ, opcodes `end`, `loop_end` |
| `filter-type` | which frequencies a filter lets through | SFZ, opcode `fil_type` |
| `enabled` | whether a part is on or off | LV2 core, designation `lv2:enabled` |
| `effect-type` | which effect, or which kind of effect, a part runs | SFZ, opcode `type` of the `<effect>` header |
| `modulation-depth` | how far the own movement of an effect goes | Novation Peak User Guide, "Chorus pages", Chorus Depth |

> NOTE The name of `filter-type` holds its values to filters: it applies to the part `filter` alone, as `delay-time` is the time of a delay.

> NOTE `enabled` and `effect-type` have no value and no maker's word. `enabled` says that a part runs. A mute says the opposite, that a part is silenced, and no term of this state names it.

| Term | A value of | Rows of the source | Maker's word |

| `sine` | `waveform` | SFZ, opcode `sample`: `*sine` | |

| `square` | `waveform` | SFZ, opcode `sample`: `*square` | |
| `noise-wave` | `waveform` | SFZ, opcode `sample`: `*noise` | |

> NOTE An SFZ filter type with a number of poles names one filter of that number, not the whole function: it is a row of the source, not a maker's word. The noise choice of a waveform selector is `noise-wave` because `noise` is the name of a part.

### 4.4 Modulation matrix and modulation destination

State 2 has no term for a modulation matrix or for a modulation destination, and they are not entered as new terms: they are the `route` and the `destination` of [draft 0021](../proposals/0021-a-source-or-destination-of-modulation-carries-its-meaning.md), which is not part of this state.

| Setting | Applies to, in addition to state 1 |
|---|---|
| `level`, `pan`, `pitch` | `track`, `sample`, each drum instrument |

> EXAMPLE
>
> ```json
> { "osc": "/filter/type", "cc": 70, "role": { "parts": [{ "part": "filter" }], "setting": "filter-type" } }
> ```

> EXAMPLE `/Reverb/Enabled`, `/Oscillator/1/Enabled`, `/Track/1/Enabled`, `/Effect/1/EffectType` and `/Chorus/ModulationDepth` are known roles of state 2.

### 6.1 The send to an effect, its level, its mix and its movement

Four settings can stand side by side on an effect, each with one meaning.

| Setting | Says | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `depth` | how much goes to the effect, its send: the meaning of state 1, unchanged | `/Reverb/Depth` |
| `mix` | how much of the effect is heard against the dry sound | `/Reverb/Mix` |
| `level` | how loud a part is, the effect's own volume included | `/Reverb/Level` |
| `modulation-depth` | how far the own movement of the effect goes | `/Chorus/ModulationDepth` |

A descriptor that names state 2 MUST write the send to an effect as `depth`.

A descriptor that names state 2 MUST write how far the own movement of an effect goes as `modulation-depth`.

> NOTE Makers often call the extent of that movement "Depth" (Novation Peak, Chorus Depth). The name differs here: `depth` keeps the meaning state 1 takes from MIDI 2.0 (M2-104-UM, table 22, Reverb Send Level and Chorus Send Level), so `/Chorus/Depth` is the send to the chorus. The depth of the vibrato stays `/Vibrato/Depth`.

> NOTE Two limits remain. `/Reverb/Depth` names the send to the reverb for the instrument: the send of one track, with a message of its own, has no role, since `reverb` stands under no part. A control that mutes a track has no role either.

## 7 The track of a role and the contexts of 0.7

A role and the contexts of [0.7](../0.7.md) can both say which track a control reaches. They share it so that it is said once.

A descriptor MUST write a control that holds for several tracks, told apart by the `in` of its ways, as one entry whose role names no track (`/Filter/Cutoff`).

A descriptor MUST write `track` in a role only for a control that reaches one given track and says it by no context: an entry per track, each with its own message (`/Track/2/Filter/Cutoff`).

A reader MUST accept a role whatever the contexts of its control: it checks nothing of this sharing, a context being a free name.

## 8 The rule of entry

A publisher MUST enter a term into the vocabulary only for a function that no existing term or composition names.

A publisher MUST NOT let the same function in twice.

A publisher MUST give each term a `source`, a document and its table (a maker's manual included), or a `shared_by`.

> NOTE The rule asks for no number of makers: a term enters on its function. The field `shared_by` keeps its form, two devices of two makers at least, and a reader refuses another.

> NOTE A reader refuses in a state what [the text of state 1](1.md), section 9, lists, with the types of section 3 above. How a term is proposed is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 9 The sources (informative)

The documents are cited, never copied. They were read on 2026-11-27.

- **SFZ format**, the pages of the opcodes `sample`, `offset`, `end`, `loop_start`, `loop_end` and `fil_type` (`sfzformat.com/opcodes/`). The waveforms are listed on the page of `sample` as sounds of the ARIA engine.
- **General MIDI System Level 1** (MIDI Association, RP-003), its percussion key map. The document is downloaded with an account of the MIDI Association; the rows were read in a public copy of the map (`musescore.org`).
- **LV2 core** (`lv2plug.in/ns/lv2core`), the designation `lv2:enabled`: "Whether processing is currently enabled (not bypassed)."
- **SFZ format**, the page of the opcode `type` of the `<effect>` header (`sfzformat.com/opcodes/type`): "Effect type".
- **Novation Peak User Guide** (`userguides.novationmusic.com`, "Novation Peak in detail"), "Chorus pages": the parameter Depth sets the amount of modulation applied to the delay time of the chorus.
- **Elektron Digitakt II** (`elektron.se/explore/digitakt-ii`): audio tracks, a slice machine.
- **Novation Circuit Rhythm** (`novationmusic.com/products/circuit-rhythm`): sample tracks, slices.
