# Governance

Open Synth Descriptors has one maintainer: Romain Peyrichou
([@roomi-fields](https://github.com/roomi-fields)). He decides what the
format is: its fields, their meaning, each new version, and what is published.

Anyone may ask a question or propose a change through the repository's
[issues](https://github.com/roomi-fields/open-synth-descriptors/issues) and
[discussions](https://github.com/roomi-fields/open-synth-descriptors/discussions);
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) says how. A published version never
changes: a change of the format is a new version, which the maintainer decides.

This repository is exported from a private workshop where the maintainer, with
the help of AI agents, develops OSD and reviews every change; a pull request is
never merged here, [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) says how it is imported.

1.0 is published when three independent readers hold the format: two readers
written without `osd`, in other languages, and a complete round trip with a
partner's format.

A vulnerability is reported privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.
Conduct follows [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
